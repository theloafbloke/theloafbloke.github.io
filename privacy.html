Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Precision Landscape & Hardscape (“we,” “us”) is an owner-run landscaping company in West Michigan. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you request an estimate, call, text, or email us, or fill out a form on our website or on one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, email address, town or address, preferred start date, and a description of your project.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, prepare your estimate, schedule and carry out work, and follow up with you about your project. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Services we rely on

A few outside services handle information on our behalf:

  • Our website form is delivered to us by Web3Forms.
  • Forms on our Facebook and Instagram ads are provided by Meta.
  • Meta’s tools help us see how our ads perform, and may use cookies or similar technology in your browser.
  • Our website loads its fonts from Google, so Google may receive your IP address when you visit.

These companies handle information under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to serve you and to maintain our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Call, text, or email us and we will take care of it.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
