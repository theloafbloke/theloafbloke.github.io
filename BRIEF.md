# Client brief: Precision Landscape & Hardscape

Source: "Website Design Ideas.pdf" from the owner (transcribed).

## Color scheme
Gray / dark gray / dark green / olive green.

## About / Homepage
- Photo of the owners, plus the story of how they started the business.
- Show their passion for the work and give customers reasons to trust them with their time and money.
- Come across as personal and caring, with high-quality work, and show how they like to interact with customers and handle their needs.
- A short background on each owner, a bit like a résumé but more personal. Highlight related past work.

## Services
Mention that they are currently getting their building license.

- Weeding, brush clearing, general seasonal yard cleanup, fall leaf cleanup
- Stone and mulch garden beds with edging installation, plus garden bed maintenance
- Light carpentry projects, raised bed builds
- Plant and tree removal and installation, seeding
- Patios and walkways
- Custom design and installation for the customer's project idea

Possible additions: power washing, Christmas light installation, gutter cleanup, general debris removal.

Include before-and-after photos of projects done so far.

## Contact
- A "Request an Estimate" button that opens a form and emails the request to them. Make clear that estimates are free.
- Form fields: Name, Phone Number, Email, Description of Project, Preferred Start Date.
- Near the button, show the other ways to reach them:
  - precisionlandscapeandhardscape@gmail.com
  - 231-740-8131
- The estimate button and contact info belong in the header and stay visible on every page.
