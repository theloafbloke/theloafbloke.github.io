About Us

Why we started Precision

We started Precision Landscape & Hardscape because we love this kind of work: turning an overgrown corner into a clean garden bed, or a muddy path into a stone walkway that will last for years. After [years] of doing landscaping for other companies and for friends and neighbors, we decided to build something of our own.

We keep things small on purpose. When you call, you talk to one of us. When we give you a quote, it's in writing and it's the price you pay. And when we're done, we walk the job with you and don't leave until you're happy with it.

Services

What we do

From a one-day fall cleanup to a new patio, every job starts with a free visit and a written estimate.

Cleanups & Brush Clearing

Weeding, brush and overgrowth clearing, spring and fall yard cleanups, and leaf removal.

Garden Beds & Edging

New stone or mulch beds with clean edging, plus ongoing bed maintenance through the season.

Raised Beds & Light Carpentry

Custom raised garden beds, planter boxes, borders and other small build projects.

Planting, Trees & Seeding

Plant and tree installation or removal, and seeding for new or patchy lawns.

Patios & Walkways

Paver and natural stone patios, walkways and steps on a properly prepared base, built to last through Michigan winters.

Custom Design & Install
